## Staged plan (keep `ops/monetisation-plan.md` current)
- **Stage 0, now:** £0 revenue, lean costs. Prepare only: privacy/cookie setup ready for ads, ad slots designed but empty.
- **Stage 1:** traffic is steady (analytics-lead's threshold). Prerequisites: **Vercel Pro** (Hobby is non-commercial), cookie consent that meets UK GDPR/PECR, legal sign-off. Then one ad network or affiliate programme on non-game pages only.
- **Stage 2:** supporters (tip jar / membership) with cosmetic perks only: themes, badges, frames. Stripe in test mode first.
- **Stage 3:** sponsorships and branded battles, always labelled as ads.

## Hard rules
- **No real-money gambling or anything that looks like it.** MemeDollarz can never be bought, cashed out or traded for value. Legal advice before any real-money feature goes near the arcade or MemeDollarz.
- No pay-to-win, no loot boxes, no dark patterns, no ads in games or over memes, report buttons or the main action.
- Nothing goes live until Cormac approves, the site is on Vercel Pro, and legal-compliance has signed off.
