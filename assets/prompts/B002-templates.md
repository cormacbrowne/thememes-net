## Rules for this batch

- **Reusable:** the pose or situation carries the joke, so one picture fits many captions.
- **Originality is the hard gate.** Each image keeps only the generic joke structure. Reject any result that recalls the source meme's character, framing or props, even if it looks good.
- **Source meme names stay out of Gemini.** Each **Avoid** line names the source meme for the curator only. The text pasted into Gemini uses the name-free **Do not include** list, so it doesn't prime the model towards the thing we're avoiding.
- **Caption space (`classic`, P02–P20):** the Forge draws captions in roughly the top and bottom 19%, and the Daily Caption Battle in the top and bottom 22%. Keep faces and key action between 22% and 78% of the height.
- **Crop:** ask for 4:3, final crop 9:8 at 1080×960. A 4:3 image loses about 8% from each side and no height, so nothing important goes near the side edges.
- **Visible Gemini mark (D-003 C1):** Cormac turns it off with Google's own setting (gemini.google.com > Settings > Media Watermark) before generating. If that setting is missing on his account, he stops and tells Max. Never crop, paint out, cover or use a remover tool on a mark.
- **Processing (D-003 C2):** images made with the mark off may be resized, cropped, converted and captioned. Keep the original PNGs, never try to remove SynthID, and test one converted WebP per batch with Gemini's verifier.
- **Human check (D-003 C4):** a person checks every image for real people, characters, brands or close recreations of famous memes.

## P01 layout check (`tpl-drake-compare`)

Checked against `buildHouseOverlay` in `supabase/functions/_shared/houseTemplates.js`:

- **House memes:** black Inter text, no outline, in boxes covering x 53–97%, y 5–45% and y 55–95%. The whole right half must be plain and light in both rows.
- **Daily Caption Battle:** `buildCaptionOverlay` in `src/utils/dailyCaptionBattle.js` ignores the layout and draws outlined Anton across the full width at the top and bottom (y 2–22% and 78–98%). P01 therefore keeps both faces close to the middle line, between 22% and 78%.
- **Style block:** P01 rewords the block so it doesn't contradict the light panels: only the left-hand pictures are dark, each has one clear subject, and the empty space is the right-hand panels.
- **Originality:** the otter faces the viewer in both rows and keeps its paws close to its body, so the grid is the only thing shared with the familiar format.
- **If the right half comes out textured or dark:** regenerate. Don't paint a panel over it.

## Curation checklist

A person checks every image. Reject for: any nameable character or likeness · a close recreation of a famous meme · the source meme's pose or framing · text or letter-like marks (check buttons, coins, crates, the cup, the car) · logos or mark-like shapes added by the picture itself · warped paws, claws or faces · subject unclear at 300 px wide · busy top or bottom band · off-palette · amber glow outside P08, P09 and P20.

Keep every original PNG. After conversion, test one WebP from the batch with Gemini's verifier and record the result.
