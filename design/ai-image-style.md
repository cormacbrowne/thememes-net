# AI image style (owner: design-director, used by image-studio)

## The look
**Original, photographic or richly illustrated meme pictures in an 80s neon cyber-arcade world.** These replace the flat pixel-graphic seed cards. They should read instantly at phone size and leave clear space for a caption.

## Style block (paste into every prompt)
> Original meme-style image, 80s retro-futurist synthwave mood, dark night scene lit by magenta-pink and cyan neon, hints of amber, soft haze, subtle film grain, high contrast, clear single subject, expressive and funny, clean composition with empty space at the top and bottom for caption text, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks.

## Rules
- **Subjects:** original characters only. Animals, robots, arcade cabinets, invented everyday people (clearly generic, not resembling anyone real), objects with personality.
- **Never:** real people or lookalikes, celebrities, politicians, copyrighted or trademarked characters (no Pepe, Doge-the-real-dog likeness, Spider-Man, Wojak, etc.), brands, logos, real products, real game footage, named artists' styles.
- **No text in the image.** Captions are added by the site's canvas code. AI text comes out garbled.
- **Caption space:** keep the top ~18% and bottom ~18% calm (sky, wall, floor, blur).
- **Shape:** 9:8 (the feed's 540×480 frame). Ask Gemini for a near-square or 4:3 landscape and crop to 9:8.
- **Colour roles still apply:** pink/cyan neon for mood; amber only where the joke is about money or winning.
- **Consistency:** same lighting family across a batch so the feed looks like one site.

## Reject if
Looks like a real or famous person · any character you could name · text or letter-like marks · a logo or watermark-like shape · warped hands/faces · cluttered (subject unclear at 300 px wide) · off-palette.
